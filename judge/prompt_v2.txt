Does the image contain visible reconstruction artefacts?

Answer Yes if you can clearly see any of these:
- floaters: semi-transparent blobs, haze, smudges or specks floating in empty space, including small dark specks in the white background
- specks: the image looks grainy or dotted, like a point cloud
- ragged edges: the outline of the object is torn or jagged, or has bits breaking off
- holes: parts of an object or surface that are missing
- broken geometry: surfaces that look melted, streaked, torn, or doubled

Answer No if the image looks like a clean photo or rendering.

Ignore mild overall softness or slight blur, colour, brightness or lighting, and what the object is or whether it looks nice. An empty white background is fine, but anything in it that should not be there counts.
